About Us

Who we are, and how it runs.

How mentors and mentees work together, and how
your year with us will look.

We get results.

Launchpad exists to help you move somewhere, from wherever you're starting, toward experience that actually counts.

  • Real experience, not just advice

    We help you get real, practical experience, the kind that's hard to land without already having it.

  • Support built for your career

    We help you build skills and instincts for consulting, finance, operations, or any other career area.

  • Help finding your way

    We'll help you find the path that fits you, even if it's not the one you expected.

How it works

You join as a mentee. Next year, you'll return as a mentor to guide a mentee of your own (and get that sweet resume experience).

Mentee

Learn from an experienced mentor. Spend the semester working toward real, practical experience.

Mentor

Guides their mentee: shares what worked for them, helps with resume/interview skills, and checks in regularly.

What a semester looks like

General timeline

  1. Now: Get matched

    You get paired with your mentor, and you catch them up on anything you've done so far.

  2. Fall/Winter: Reach out, with support

    Companies, student orgs, small local businesses. You'll reach out yourself, with your mentor pulling strings where they can.

  3. Entire year: Build real skills as you go

    Resume tips, interview practice, and any skills to help you ahead of the competition.

  4. Next fall: Return as a mentor

    Come back and teach a mentee all you've learned, and put that experience on your resume.
